## Phase 4 design decisions

### DD-M8-P4-1: Designation-based sanctioning routes — role-only in v1

**Decision:** The §11.10 sanctioning matrix distinguishes Professor / Associate Professor / Lecturer-tier by designation. In v1 (pre-M10 Faculty module), these are represented as **roles** rather than designations on a Faculty record:
- A Professor-level user holds the `PROFESSOR` role (level 75) in addition to `FACULTY`.
- An Associate Professor holds `ASSOC_PROFESSOR` (level 73) in addition to `FACULTY`.
- Lecturer-tier and other departmental staff hold only `FACULTY` (level 30), which routes to Director of Campus per §22.I.iii.

The `applicant_designation_regex` column on `LeaveSanctionAuthorityRule` is retained in the schema but set to `null` on every v1 rule. M10 Faculty module may rework designation-bearing rules; the YAML rewrite at that point is trivial.

Seeded roles: `PROFESSOR` (level 75), `ASSOC_PROFESSOR` (level 73) added to `scripts/seed.py` in Phase 4.

### DD-M8-P4-2: 20-employee synthetic fixture placement

The §11.16 acceptance fixture (`tests/fixtures/leave_20_employees.py`) is NOT loaded by `scripts/seed.py`. It is opt-in — imported and called by integration and acceptance tests only. The 20 representative employees cover every §11 scenario: vacation/non-vacation, teaching/admin, men/women, new joiners/established staff, honorary/superannuated/visiting fellows. All usernames prefixed `m8test_` for grep-clean teardown.

### DD-M8-P4-3: YAML rule count (73)

The full sanctioning matrix encodes 73 rules: 14 CL (§22.I) + 1 SCL (§22.II) + 14×4 EL/HPL/CML/ML (§23) + 2 EOL/SL (§24). All rules have `applicant_designation_regex: null` (v1 role-only matching). CL lacks a DEPUTY_REGISTRAR entry because §22 does not separately enumerate sub-Registrar staff; they are office staff covered by the REGISTRAR_OFFICE rule.
